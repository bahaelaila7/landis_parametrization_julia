## Known limitations / follow-ups
- Pan coverage: GA+FL ecoregions (Region 4 pending); other plots are `out_of_region` (FVSjl still works there).
- Pan volumes/board feet are approximations (BA × H × 0.42; 4 bd ft/ft³ for QMD ≥ 9 in); no dead-wood carbon pools.
- HBAD: shade-tolerant recruits do not build an understory under a closed canopy (self-thinning removes the smallest
  cohorts first); a size-threshold for self-thinning would fix it but changes the calibrated model — left as an option to add.
- PnET has no establishment (planting works).
- The climate baseline has no warming trend by design; warming comes from scenarios.
- TPA/BA observations are not yet in any calibration loss — they would resolve HBAD's growth/mortality/density equifinality.

## Addendum (end of run)
- **Region 4 parameter sets** (`results/params_yaml/r4_{biomass,pnet,hbad}_v1.yaml`, all 30k R4 plots, same procedures as
  GA+FL; in-sample only): ±5/10/20 % pass of 50 groups — HBAD 45/49/49, PnET 42/47/47, Biomass 44/46/49. They are now the
  worker's defaults (the GA+FL sets remain as cross-validated, region-specific samples). Logs: `results/r4/`.
- **PnET alternatives**: anchored exploration found no distinct behavioural PnET sets — with start noise all candidates had
  12–28 groups out of tolerance; starting exactly at the anchor, every move of a species-level trait broke some group (the
  traits couple many groups; block-wise acceptance cannot coordinate the compensating FolN moves). The PnET alternatives are
  therefore independent gdigel optimize-mode solutions from random starts (cmaes-gafl run): held-out on par with a train-only
  reference fit; 100-yr biomass CV ≈ 15 % across the sets (vs 6.6 % HBAD, 1.7 % Biomass Succession).
- **Bug found by the API test and fixed**: HBAD priors are module state; with several parameter sets loaded in one worker a
  run could use another set's species list → per-run configuration (`run_scenario`).
